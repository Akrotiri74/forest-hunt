🌲 Forest Hunt

Choose your team.

Do not start moving yet.

You will have 60 minutes to complete as many challenges as possible and return to the meeting point.

  • Your first challenge is your team's mandatory starting challenge.
  • Once your starting challenge is completed, the other challenges unlock.
  • You can then complete the remaining challenges in any order.
  • Some challenges require a photograph.
  • Some challenges require you to find something and solve a puzzle.
  • Photographs may take a little time to be checked. You can continue playing while they are being checked.
  • Each challenge is worth a different number of points.
  • You must return to the meeting point before finishing.
  • For every 2 minutes or part of 2 minutes that you are late, 5 points are deducted.

Maximum strategy: decide whether the points available at a distant challenge are worth the time it takes to get there and back.

When everyone has been briefed and is ready, the organiser will start the hunt.
