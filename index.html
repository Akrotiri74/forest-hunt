THE HUNT

You have 60 minutes to complete as many challenges as possible and return to the meeting point.

Your first challenge is compulsory and will send your team in a different direction from the other teams.

Once your first challenge is completed, all remaining challenges become available and you may tackle them in any order.

Some challenges require photographs. These will be checked by the organiser.

Other challenges require you to find something and solve a puzzle using information from the physical location.

Most points wins.

If you return late, points will be deducted.
